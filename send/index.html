Send a case

Your first case, designed free.

Three short steps: check the files, fill a one-minute prescription, and send. We confirm receipt and return the design in 24–48 hours.

1Files we need

Anonymized files only — use a case code, never the patient's name.

Scans

  • Prepared arch (STL / PLY)
  • Antagonist
  • Bite registration or buccal scan
  • Or a complete exocad project (.dentalProject)

Implant cases

  • Implant system and platform
  • Scan body used
  • Ti-base / link, if you have a preference
  • Screw-retained or cemented

Your preferences

  • Material and shade
  • Cement gap and minimum thickness
  • Connector size for bridges
  • Tooth library, if you use one

2Digital prescription

Fill it in, then email it, send it by WhatsApp, or save it as PDF and attach it to your files.

Tap the teeth involved (FDI numbering)

Nothing is uploaded or stored by this page — it only prepares the message for you.

3Send the files

Use whatever is easiest for you.

Email attachment

Up to ~25 MB: attach the files to the prescription email to boundlesscad.design@gmail.com.

Download link

Larger cases: share a WeTransfer, Google Drive or Dropbox link in the email.

Your portal

Already use a case portal or cloud folder? Invite our email and tell us the case code.

Not sure what to send?

Write to us with a screenshot of the case — we'll tell you exactly what we need.
