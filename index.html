How we work

Send your scan. Get a design you can mill.

A workflow built for busy labs: no software to install, no staff to train, no minimum volume.

01

Send your case

Email your scans (STL / PLY or an exocad project) with the prescription, or share a download link.

02

We design in exocad

Anatomy, contacts, occlusion and connectors — set to your material, cement gap and CAM setup.

Screenshots or an exocad webview file. Revisions are included until you approve.
