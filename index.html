Quality control

Every design is checked before it reaches your mill.

Our studio is led by a dentist. Every case goes through the same clinical checklist, so what looks right on screen also fits in the mouth.

Checkpoints on every crown and bridge
  1. Margin lineContinuous, following the preparation, with no over- or under-extension.
  2. Proximal contactsTight enough to hold floss, loose enough to seat without adjustment.
  3. OcclusionStatic and dynamic contacts checked against the antagonist.
  4. Minimum thicknessSet to the minimum values for the material you mill.
  5. Emergence profileNatural, cleansable contours, especially on implant crowns.
  6. Insertion path & connectorsOne path without undercuts; connector sizes to the material.

Something is not right? Revisions are included until you approve the design.
