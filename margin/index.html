Questions

Are these real patient scans?

No. The three preparations are generated by the game, with realistic scan noise added. No patient data is used, and nothing you do is uploaded.

How is the score calculated?

Your points are joined into a closed line. At every degree around the tooth we measure the 3D distance between your line and the true margin of the model. The score falls as the mean deviation grows, with a smaller penalty for the worst spot and for gaps where the line is not covered.

Is this how a margin is judged clinically?

It is a game. It trains the eye for finish lines on a scan; clinical margin detection depends on the real die, the scan quality and the material.
